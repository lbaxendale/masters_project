About

Nutrifem was created to support women's hormonal and menstrual health through nutritional therapy, including women with PCOS who may suffer from various health issues such as hormonal imbalance, insulin resistance, and high blood pressure.

How does Nutrifem work?

Nutrition recommendations are based on nutritional, clinical, and academic literature in combination with machine learning. *PLEASE NOTE: Recommendations provided are not medical advice. Consult a licensed health professional for treatement of health conditions.

Nutrifem serves as a wellness companion rather than a medical advice, recommending food based on a user questionnaire and educating them on the nutrients of foods. Simply answer a series of questions, and receive recommendations instantly.
